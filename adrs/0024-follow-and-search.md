# 0024 What refs and search take and show

2026-09-27. `shop-knol refs <locator> --outbound|--inbound [--via FIELD] [--type TYPE] [--depth N]` makes one Refs call; one of the two directions is required, and with no `--depth` it asks for one step, since kb v0.2.0 reaches nothing at depth 0. Its answer is a sequence, nearest first as kb gives it, each entry the reached artifact's `id`, `type` and `title`, `via` (the link of the last step), its stub's fields, and `route`, the steps taken as `field` and `id` each. `shop-knol search <text> [--type TYPE] [--in sections|fields|all]` makes one Search call, `sections` when `--in` is not given; its answer is a sequence in kb's order, each entry `id`, `type`, `title`, then `section` or `field`, whichever matched, and `snippet`. Neither has a wrapper key, as `list` has none.
