# 0058 A decision links to its shop; a shop's decisions are inferred

2026-10-08. A decision carries a required link to the shop it belongs to, for reference at a glance and for querying; a shop's decisions are found through the links pointing at it, and `shop.decisions` goes. A link is stored in one direction only, since kb answers the other through its links both ways. The approved scenarios that see what a decision points at gain its shop.
