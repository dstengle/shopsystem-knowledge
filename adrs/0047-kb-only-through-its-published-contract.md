# 0047 shop-knowledge knows kb only through what kb publishes

2026-09-27. No code of shop-knowledge, under `src/` or `tests/`, knows anything of kb but what kb publishes (kb adrs/0018): `kb.contract`, `kb.client.connect`, `kb.content`, and fault `rule` names. No step reads or writes a knowledge base's files, imports another kb module, replaces kb's clock, names kb's storage, or spells kb's fault wording. Where a scenario needs kb in a state no contract call can produce (a stored file damaged, an artifact unfit for its type, a link pointing at nothing, a history on a given day), a stand-in for kb at the contract boundary gives kb's contract messages for that state; how kb comes to give them is kb's to prove in its own suite. Every other scenario runs against the real kb. No test reaches a knowledge base outside its own temporary directory. Supersedes the spec's "never mocked" and CLAUDE.md's leave for steps to hand-edit a knowledge base's files.
