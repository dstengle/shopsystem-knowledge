# 0072 An artifact's id names its owner, product and bounded context

2026-10-09. Every artifact's namespace names its owner, its product and its bounded context, in that order, so that the shopsystem product's own contexts and the products it hosts (ecommercesite) never share a name, within one organisation or across several. The levels are shop-knowledge's convention; kb sees only the namespace. The user's decision.
