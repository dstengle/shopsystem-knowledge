# 0077 A migration leaves out what needs judgement and lists it

2026-10-10. `shop-knol migrate` drafts everything it can read from the markdown mechanically and leaves out whatever needs judgement (a behaviour line's title, a gist, a scenario's line where the counts disagree, an ADR for a decision that has none), writing beside the batches a list of each gap with the IRI, the place, what is needed and the source text to work from. Nothing is guessed, so nothing guessed lands; an empty list means the batches are ready to apply. The user's decision.
